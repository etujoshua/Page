About Page & Terms

Last updated: November 2026.

1. About Page

Page is a digital bookstore. Books are priced and bought with PAGE tokens, which work like store credit inside Page. People earn PAGE tokens as a reading reward and can also get tokens from other users in the Auction House. Page does not offer banking, lending, deposit or investment services.

2. Eligibility and accounts

You must give a true date of birth when you register. Anyone under 18 may read and earn tokens (Mining-Only Mode) but may not buy tokens, bid, or sell until their 18th birthday. You are responsible for your account and password. Registration is free. One person, one account.

3. PAGE tokens

PAGE tokens are units of in-platform credit used to buy books and to trade between users. They are not money, not a security, not a deposit and not an investment. Page does not promise that tokens will keep, gain or regain any value, and does not promise to buy tokens back. The total supply is fixed at 10,000,000 PAGE. Tokens from deleted accounts go back into the pool that readers can earn.

4. Reading rewards

Reading a book while it is open earns a reward of PAGE tokens worth about US$1 per hour at the current token price, shared out by time spent reading. Rewards are a gift from the platform, are not wages, can change as the price or number of users changes, and stop when the pool is fully distributed. Mining starts only after the launch target is reached.

5. Launch stages

Before launch you can register, invite friends and publish books. When the pre-registration target is reached, reading rewards and book purchases open. After a 14-day mining period the Auction House opens. Page may adjust these stages if needed and will tell users.

6. Token price

The displayed PAGE price follows a formula based on the number of users, the share of tokens in circulation and recent buying and selling activity. It is informational, can rise or fall quickly, and is not advice to buy or sell.

7. Auction House

The Auction House lets users sell tokens to other users. Page provides the marketplace and does not guarantee a buyer. Tokens you list are held in escrow until sold or withdrawn. Buyers are filled at the current price, oldest listing first, up to 833 PAGE per hour. When demand in an hour is at least 1.5 times what is for sale, an hourly bid round runs: the highest bids win, and each winner pays the price they bid. If there are enough tokens for every bid, bids are filled at the current price. Page charges the seller a 17% fee on every successful sale.

8. Books and authors

Authors set a price in a supported currency (minimum about US$0.50). Page adds a 33.6% platform margin to the price shown to readers, so the author receives their full asking price and Page keeps the margin. You may only publish material you own or have the right to sell. Page may remove content that is unlawful or infringing.

9. Payments

Cash payments are made in US-dollar stablecoins through MiniPay, a third-party wallet. Page does not hold your cash. Payments on a blockchain are final and cannot be reversed by Page. Network fees and MiniPay's own terms apply. You must give a correct MiniPay address to receive payments.

10. Receipts

Each purchase, sale or listing creates a receipt that lists the serial of each token involved. Keep your receipts.

11. Referrals

You may share your referral link. Fake or automated sign-ups are not allowed and may lead to account removal.

12. Rules

No fraud, no attempts to copy or forge tokens, no tampering with the platform or other users' accounts, no automated reading to farm rewards, no illegal content.

13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the sidebar. Deletion is permanent: your tokens go back to the pool for others to earn, and you must register a new account to return.

14. Risks

Digital tokens can lose all value. Websites, wallets and networks can fail. Only use tokens and money you can afford to lose. Laws about digital tokens differ by country and may change; you are responsible for following the rules where you live, including any taxes.

15. Liability

Page is provided "as is". To the extent the law allows, Page and its owner are not liable for lost tokens, lost profits, price changes or losses caused by third-party services.

16. Updates and contact

Page may change these terms or the platform and will show an update notice. Using Page after a change means you accept it. Contact: etujoshua80@gmail.com.
